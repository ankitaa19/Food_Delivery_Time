GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your delivery estimate.

How is the delivery time estimated? +

The estimate considers factors such as restaurant preparation time, distance, traffic, weather, time of day and delivery partner experience.

Is the predicted time exact? +

No. The result is an estimated delivery time. Actual delivery can vary because real-world conditions can change during the journey.

Why does traffic affect delivery time? +

Higher traffic can increase the amount of time required to travel from the restaurant to the destination.

Does weather affect the estimate? +

Yes. Weather conditions such as rain, fog or other difficult conditions can affect delivery time.

What information do I need to provide? +

You only need to provide the order distance, preparation time, delivery partner experience, weather, traffic, time of day and vehicle type.

Can the actual delivery time be different? +

Yes. The prediction provides an estimate and actual delivery time may vary depending on changing conditions.
