QUESTIONS

FAQs

Answers about the estimate, the inputs, and how close the prediction usually is.

What does the estimate include?

Preparation plus the ride, adjusted for distance, traffic, weather, time of day, vehicle, and rider experience.

Which values can I choose?

Weather is Clear, Rainy, Snowy, Foggy, or Windy. Traffic is Low, Medium, or High. Time of day is Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night. Vehicles are Bike, Scooter, or Car.

Why do distance and preparation time matter most?

A longer trip and a longer kitchen wait both add minutes before the order can reach you. The model uses both directly.

How do traffic and weather change the time?

Heavy traffic slows the rider. Rain, snow, fog, and wind can also add time compared with a clear evening on a quiet road.

How close is the prediction?

The production model is a linear regression with about 5.9 minutes of average error on held-out orders.
